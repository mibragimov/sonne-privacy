# Sonne: Workout Journal — Support

Sonne is a plain-text workout journal for iPhone. Each day is one page:
you write on it however you like, and the app keeps out of the way.
There are no exercise databases, no set pickers and no charts — just the
page, a checklist key and a timer.

from inside the app: **More → Settings → About → Support**.

## Common questions

### How do I write a workout?

Open today's page and tap it — the page is the editor. Type anything. A
line that starts with `#` becomes an exercise heading, and lines that
start with a number are set in a mono font so loads line up. The circle
key above the keyboard starts a checklist line; tap the circle on the
page to tick it. Swipe left and right to move between days, or tap the
calendar icon to jump to a date.

### How do I reuse last week's session?

Keep your workouts as templates in **More → Templates**. On an empty day,
tap **Start from template**; on a day you have already started, use
**More → Use template…**. Either way the app copies the last day you
trained that template, with ticked boxes unticked — or the template
itself the first time. To see what you did last time without copying
it, put the caret on an exercise line and tap **history** above the
keyboard; tap a result to go to that day.

### The timer

Tap the timer icon at the top of the page, pick a duration and start
it. While it runs, the countdown replaces the icon; tap it to pause,
adjust or stop. The timer also appears on the Lock Screen and in the
Dynamic Island. At zero the app plays a chime.

If nothing appears on the Lock Screen, or the timer makes no sound when
the app is not in front, allow Live Activities and notifications for
Sonne in the iPhone's Settings → Sonne.

### Does my journal sync between devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud account — nothing passes through a server
run by the developer. Sign the iPhone into iCloud and leave iCloud Drive
on. **Settings → Sync** in the app shows what it sees on the **iCloud**
row, and **Days written** counts the days that have something on them.
Without iCloud the app still works fully; the journal simply stays on
the one device.

### How do I get my writing out?

**More → Share day** hands one day's text to the share sheet as plain
text, and **Settings → Export → Export all notes** exports the whole
journal as a single Markdown file. Your pages are stored as plain
strings and are never parsed into anything else, so what you get out is
what you typed.

### How do I delete a day, or everything?

**More → Clear day** removes everything written on that day. It asks
first, and it can't be undone. Deleting the app removes everything it
stored on the device. Anything synced can be removed from the iPhone's
Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Sonne.

## Privacy

Sonne has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no
