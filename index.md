# Privacy Policy

_Last updated 30 September 2026._

This app is a plain-text workout journal. It is built so that your data
stays yours.

## What the app stores

- The page you write for each day, the templates you create, and the
  dates attached to them.
- A handful of preferences (timer defaults, whether the app has shown
  its welcome page).

Everything above is stored on your device. If your device is signed in
to iCloud, the same data is synchronised to your private iCloud database
through Apple's CloudKit, so it appears on your other devices and
survives a lost phone. Only you can read that database; the developer
cannot.

## What the app does not do

- No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
- No analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising identifiers, no
  tracking of any kind.
- No servers run by the developer. The app never sends your data
  anywhere except your own iCloud.
- No third-party services.

## Your data, your way

Any day can be shared as plain text from the More menu, and the whole
journal can be exported as a single Markdown file from Settings. Deleting
the app deletes its local data; data in iCloud can be removed from
Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage on your device.
